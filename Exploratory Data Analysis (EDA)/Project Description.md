# 👉College Campus Insights - EDA

### Skills:
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Data Cleaning, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), Statistical Analysis 

### Tool:
Jupyter NoteBook

### Aim:
To analyze top-ranked B.Tech, Medical, and MBA colleges from Shiksha.com based on fees, placements, and ratings, in order to derive insights about affordability, career prospects, and overall student satisfaction. 

### Steps: 
Performed data cleaning, outlier detection, and preprocessing; analyzed distributions and trends in fees, salaries, and ratings; visualized patterns using statistical plots; and summarized key insights for students and institutions.

### Approach:
 •Data Preparation – Loaded and explored raw college data (CSV format). 
 •Data Cleaning  – Handled missing values, outliers (IQR), type conversions, and incomplete rows.
 •Exploratory Analysis – Analyzed fees, salaries, placements, and rating metrics (univariate & bivariate). 
 •Visualization – Built histograms, boxplots, scatter plots, and heatmaps to reveal key patterns.
 •Statistical Insights – Identified distributions, correlations, and drivers of placement outcomes.

### Results:
Analyzed and cleaned a dataset of the top 100 NIRF-ranked colleges, uncovering strong correlations between fees, salary packages, and overall institutional ratings. Derived key affordability and ROI insights to support data-driven decision-making for students evaluating college options.
